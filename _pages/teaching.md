# Books in progress reviewer

As a reviewer for Manning Publications, I review works in progress at many different stages. I am generally recruited by Manning editors to be a reviewer for a specific book, either as an expert (e.g. data science/ML/AI books) or as a competent novice (e.g. software engineering books). I am also recruited to review book proposals, generally as an expert reviewer.  The following published books list me in the acknowledgements section as a reviewer.
