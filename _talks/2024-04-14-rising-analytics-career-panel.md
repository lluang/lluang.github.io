Panel was part of the Rising Analytics Professionals Workshop. This workshop is designed especially for those new to the analytics profession, including students, recent graduates, and those transitioning careers. Sessions include a soft-skills workshop, followed by a diverse panel of professionals who will share their career journeys, and offer candid insights about working in analytics.

Moderator: Carol DeZwarte
Warren Hearnes, PhD (Former Chief Data Scientist at Best Buy); Louis Luangkesorn (Lead Data Scientist at Highmark Health); Xiaonan (Shannon) Shang (Data Scientist and Analytics Professional); Heather Moe (Principal Product Engineer at Esri); Josh Hoskinson (Data Scientist at Chevron)
