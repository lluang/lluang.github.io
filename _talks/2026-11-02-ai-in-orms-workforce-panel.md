Panel discussion examining whether artificial intelligence serves as a threat, tool, or career accelerator within the operations research and management sciences workforce. Discussion covered experiences using AI in industrial settings including benefits in modeling and implementation, limits to usefulness (such as genericy), and fears in use (such as changing unit test conditions)

Moderator Panelist
Warren Hearnes, OptiML AI; Vinod Cheriyan, Stitch Fix; Louis Luangkesorn, Highmark Health; Carlos Zetina, FICO Xpress Optimization; Francisco Zenteno Smith, American Airlines.
