Simulation and queuing analysis evaluating physician staffing levels required to deliver timely telestroke consults across a regional hospital network. This work was used to evaluate telestroke staffing supporting community hospitals across western Pennsylvania and evaluated the impact of changing stroke protocols on emergency care.
