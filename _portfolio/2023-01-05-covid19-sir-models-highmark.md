This presentation, titled *“ACCEL Tech Talk: Solving the two population SIR model to provide peak and duration of COVID-19,”* outlines how Highmark Health utilized mathematical Susceptible-Infectious-Recovered (SIR) compartmental models to anticipate patient surges and manage regional healthcare capacity throughout Pennsylvania during the COVID-19 pandemic.
