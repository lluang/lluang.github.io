This feature highlights a multi-year collaborative research and operational improvement project led by University of Pittsburgh industrial engineers (Assistant Professor Louis Luangkesorn and Professor Jayant Rajgopal) alongside Children’s Hospital of Pittsburgh of UPMC to reduce Emergency Department (ED) wait times and optimize patient flow.
