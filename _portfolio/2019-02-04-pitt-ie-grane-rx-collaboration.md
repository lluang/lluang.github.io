This article features senior capstone projects advised by Assistant Professor Louis Luangkesorn partnering with institutional pharmacy supplier Grane Rx. 

To support Grane Rx’s expansion of PACE pharmacy services, the undergraduate student team designed production scheduling and overtime cost-optimization models. Winning first place in Industrial Engineering at the Swanson School of Engineering’s Fall 2018 Design Expo, the project delivered scalable decision-support models for live operations.
