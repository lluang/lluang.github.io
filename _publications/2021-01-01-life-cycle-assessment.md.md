In this study, associative digital technologies, machine learning, and simulation are used to process both operational and related economic data from an actual poultry farm. The objective is to quickly assess and predict the inherent reliability of long-term egg production operations based on the egg production life cycle. First, a machine learning algorithm is applied to forecast egg trade prices between poultry farms and egg dealers based on historical economic data. Then, designed simulation models are applied along with collected operational data to assess the inherent reliability of historical life cycles. As a result, machine learning and simulation technological advancements are powerful and effective for associative utilization of large volumes of both related economic and operational data.
